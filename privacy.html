Privacy policy

GTA 6 Hub analytics · last updated 22 November 2026

GTA 6 Hub analytics is a private tool that reads the YouTube analytics of the GTA 6 Hub channels for the person who runs them. It uses YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy also apply when you sign in to it

What it can see

When you sign in with Google and allow access, it can read your channel's name, handle and ID, and your channel's YouTube Analytics reports, such as views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and the countries your viewers are in. The access is read-only (Google's youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly permissions). It can't upload, change or delete anything, and it can't see your email, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account

How the data is used

The numbers are only used to make performance reports for the channel owner, so they can see which videos work and plan new ones. The owner's own scripts and AI assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic) read them to write those reports. Apart from that, nothing is sold, used for advertising or shared with anyone

Where it's kept

Signing in saves a read-only access key on the owner's own computer. The analytics numbers are kept in the owner's private files for as long as they help with planning, then deleted. The tool doesn't set cookies or store anything in your browser

Removing access

You can remove its access at any time on your Google Account's security settings page. After that it can't read anything more and the saved key stops working. The numbers stored from your channel are then deleted within 7 days
